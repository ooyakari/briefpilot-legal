Delete account

In the app

  1. Open BriefPilot.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and permanently, together with all letters, tasks and reminders on this device. To remove letters from another device, delete the app there. Please cancel an active subscription separately in your Apple ID settings or Google Play.

Without the app

Email support@giran.de from the address of your account with the subject "Delete account". We delete the account within 7 days and confirm it to you.
