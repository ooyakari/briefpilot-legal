2. What this is about

BriefPilot explains letters from authorities, landlords and companies in your language, creates tasks, calendar entries and reminders and helps you write replies. Letters often contain sensitive data, so BriefPilot is built to keep as much data as possible on your device.

3. Data we process

  • Account: user ID, email address and, if shared, your name from Sign in with Apple or Google (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Photos of your letters: processed only on your device (on-device text recognition with Apple Vision or Google ML Kit). Photos are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Letter text and analysis: the recognised text, explanation, tasks and deadlines are stored only on your device, not on our servers. They are not synced between devices; if you delete the app they are gone (unless included in a backup of your device).
  • AI analysis: before any text is sent to our AI provider, the app removes names, addresses, bank details, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth and ID numbers on your device and replaces them with placeholders. Only this anonymised text is sent to the DeepSeek API (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd., China) and it is not stored by us after the answer. This only happens after your explicit consent in the app (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy; after that no letter text is sent. The automatic removal is carefully built but cannot guarantee that every personal detail is detected.
  • Reminders: scheduled as local notifications on your device; no data is sent to us for this.
  • Subscription: purchase status and expiry via RevenueCat. Payment data is handled only by Apple or Google.
  • Technical logs: for security and abuse prevention (e.g. number of analyses per day), Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

We do not use advertising or tracking and we never sell data.

4. Recipients and processors

  • Supabase Inc. – sign-in, subscription status and server functions (no letter content); data stored in Frankfurt am Main (EU).
  • DeepSeek – AI analysis of anonymised text (China), see above.
  • RevenueCat Inc. (USA) – subscription management; EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Apple Inc. / Google LLC – sign-in and payments.

5. Retention

Letters, tasks and reminders stay only on your device until you delete them or the app. Your account exists until you delete it; deleting your account immediately and permanently removes the account data on the server and all letters on this device. Purchase records of your subscription are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own legal obligations; they contain no letter content. Logs are deleted after 30 days at the latest.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection and withdrawal of consent (Art. 15–21, Art. 7(3) GDPR). Contact info@giran.de. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

7. Deleting your account

In the app under Settings → Delete account, or as described here.

8. Children

BriefPilot is intended for people aged 16 and over.

9. No legal advice

BriefPilot explains letters with the help of artificial intelligence and does not replace legal advice.
